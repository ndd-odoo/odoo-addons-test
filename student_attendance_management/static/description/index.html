Objectives & Key Results - OKRs

Helps organizations effectively implement the Objectives and Key Results (OKRs) management system, enhancing transparency and clarity in managing and measuring performance.

Key Features

Manage individual & organizational OKRs.

Visualize objectives & key results with OKRs chart.

Track objectives progress automatically.

Configure Completion Thresholds for Objectives.

Ready to integrate with other applications.

Benefits

Clear objective linkage: Connect personal, department, and company objectives, helping everyone work towards the same goal.
Enhance individual contribution: Help employees understand their role, increase motivation, and improve work efficiency.
Long-term strategy: Support building and tracking long-term strategies, helping sustainable growth.
Easy progress tracking: Automatically measure and update goal progress based on key results, enhancing management efficiency.
Visual data: OKR diagrams provide transparency in progress, aiding quick and accurate decision-making.

1. Manage Individual & Organizational OKRs

Create, edit, and track OKRs at the individual, department, and organizational levels. Link personal objectives to the organization’s goals to ensure strategic alignment.

2. Visualize objectives & key results with OKRs chart

Supports building a tree structure for OKRs, visually displaying the relationships between objectives and key results.

3. Track objectives progress automatically

Automatically calculates progress based on real-time updates of key results.

4. Configure Completion Thresholds for Objectives

Allows users to set two completion levels for each objective or key result (Key Result): Expectation (minimum required level, e.g., 70%) and Commitment (ideal target level, e.g., 100%). This feature helps clearly define the minimum acceptable performance and the maximum desired goal, supporting accurate evaluations of individual, departmental, or organizational performance
